Nairobi

Nairobi is the capital city and largest urban center of Kenya, renowned as the "Green City in the Sun." Founded in 1899 as a simple rail depot on the Uganda Railway, it quickly grew to replace Mombasa as the capital in 1907. Today, Nairobi serves as a major commercial and financial hub in East Africa, uniquely hosting the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) and a major national park right within its city limits.
