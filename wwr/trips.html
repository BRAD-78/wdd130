Featured River Experiences

4 hours · Beginner-friendly

Half Day Adventure

A lively introduction to whitewater rafting, combining time to learn the paddling basics with stretches of rapids and calmer water.

Your guide leads an equipment briefing and practice session before the river section. A good choice for friends and first-time rafters looking for an active morning or afternoon.

US$65 per person

7 hours · Intermediate

Full Day Adventure

Spend more time on the river with a varied route of rapids, scenic reaches, and a riverside lunch stop.

This longer outing suits visitors who want a fuller paddling experience. Guided preparation, equipment, and lunch are included; tell us about your group when arranging the trip.

US$110 per person
